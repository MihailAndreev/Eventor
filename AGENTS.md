# Eventor App
    - Eventor is a Next.js  + Expo app for groups of people who plan, organize and join shared events. The app is suitable for friends, colleagues, clubs, hobby groups, hiking groups, study groups, family groups and communities with recurring activities
    - Workspace 'eventor-web': Next.js-based back-end + Web front-end
    - Workspace 'eventor-mobile': Expo-based mobile client app
